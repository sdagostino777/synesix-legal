What Synesix does

It reads a lab report and tells you what each value means: the name of the test, the number, the unit, the reference range and whether that value is inside or outside it.

What it does not do

It does not diagnose, does not tell you what to do and does not recommend treatments, medicines, doses, diets or supplements. It gives no health score and does not assess your condition. It does not replace a doctor's opinion.

What a range is worth

A value inside the range is not an absolution, and a value outside it is not a diagnosis: the range is the reference the laboratory printed on that report, and comparing against that reference does not say what it means for you.

Ranges differ from laboratory to laboratory. When the report does not print one, Synesix uses a general reference and says so, because it is not the same thing.

Automatic reading can be wrong

The text is recognised from a photo, a screenshot or a PDF. A blurred photo or a multi-column table can produce a wrong number.

Check that the numbers match the report: the app asks you to, at the top of the results, shows you the line it read next to every value and lets you correct everything by hand.

Where your data lives

On this phone, in the app's private folder, protected by iOS encryption. Synesix has no server, no account and sends nothing anywhere.

In an emergency

If you feel unwell, or a value worries you, talk to your doctor. In an emergency call your country's emergency services. Synesix cannot call them for you and cannot recognise a dangerous situation.
