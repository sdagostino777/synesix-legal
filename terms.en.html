1. What this document is

These terms govern the use of Synesix, the iPhone app that reads a lab report and explains its values. By installing and using the app you accept them. If you do not accept them, delete the app.

2. What you can do

Synesix is for personal use. You can use it to read your reports, to reread values over time and to export your data whenever you want. You can install it on every device you control with the same App Store account.

3. What it is not

Synesix is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. It does not diagnose, does not indicate treatments, does not suggest medicines, doses, diets or supplements and gives no health score. Decisions about your health are yours to take with your doctor.

Do not use Synesix to make urgent decisions: the app is not designed to recognise a dangerous situation and cannot call emergency services for you.

4. Automatic reading can be wrong

The report text is recognised automatically from a photo, a screenshot or a PDF, and recognition can be wrong: a blurred photo, a crease or a multi-column table can produce a wrong number.

That is why the app asks you to check the numbers against the report, shows you the line it read next to every value, lets you correct any value by hand and keeps that correction. The paper report or the lab PDF remain the authoritative source: Synesix is a tool for reading them, not a replacement.

5. Your data is yours

The data is yours. It stays on your device and we neither see nor process it. Export as JSON or CSV is free and stays free, including for users who never buy PRO.

If you delete the app, the data goes with it: keep an export if you need one.

6. Free and PRO

Synesix is free. The free plan has no limit on the number of reports and never expires, and every value of a report is always visible, with its range and its status, even without paying.

PRO does not change what you see: it unlocks the explanation of every value, the trend charts, the PDF report for your appointment, the comparison between reports and the app lock. PRO comes in two forms, with the same features: the one-time purchase or the yearly subscription.

  • Synesix PRO forever — €69.99 once. It is a non-consumable purchase: you pay once and it stays yours, it does not renew and there is nothing to cancel. It gives the same features as the subscription, with no expiry.
  • Synesix PRO yearly — €24.99 per year. It is a subscription with automatic renewal.

The subscription renews automatically at the end of every period, at the same price, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it expires. The renewal is charged to your App Store account in the 24 hours before the end of the current period.

How to cancel the subscription: from the App Store subscription settings on your device — Settings → your name → Subscriptions — then choose Synesix and cancel. Cancelling leaves PRO access active until the end of the period you have already paid for: the current period is not refunded. If you cancel and change your mind, you can turn it back on from the same screen as long as the period has not ended.

Payment is charged to your App Store account when you confirm the purchase. The one-time purchase does not renew and there is nothing to cancel. Refunds follow Apple's rules: you ask Apple, not us, and Apple decides.

The prices shown are VAT included and are the prices for Italy. They may change for new purchases; for subscriptions already running, Apple's rules on telling you about price changes apply.

If you change phone, the “Restore purchases” button brings back the PRO you have already paid for, without paying again.
