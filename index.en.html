Synesix

Reads a lab report and tells you what each value means: the name of the test, the number, the unit, the reference range and whether it is inside or outside. It also prepares the questions to take to your doctor.

It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, does not indicate treatments and gives no health score. Your reports stay on your iPhone: no server, no account, no advertising.
