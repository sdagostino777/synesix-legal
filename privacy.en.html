Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

1. In one sentence

Synesix has no server, makes no network requests and transmits your reports to nobody. What you load stays on your iPhone, in the app's private folder.

Synesix is developed by one person, not by a company. There is no data protection officer other than the controller, because there is no systematic processing to oversee.

3. What data the app handles

No personal data is collected by us. We have no database, no server, and we receive nothing from this app.

The data the app keeps on your device is what you load:

  • the images and PDFs of the reports you choose to analyse;
  • the text recognised from those reports, line by line;
  • the values read: analyte name, number, unit, reference range and status;
  • the corrections you make by hand and the notes you write;
  • your year of birth and biological sex, if you choose to give them, for the reference ranges of a few tests;
  • your preferences: theme, language, reminder.

This data does not leave the device. We do not see it, do not read it and cannot recover it: if you delete the app, it goes with it.

4. Health data

Reports and values are data concerning health, and as such they receive the highest protection under the GDPR (Art. 9). Processing happens only on your device, at your request and for the purpose you expect: reading a report you loaded yourself.

There is no transfer to third parties, no processing by us, and no legal basis other than your consent, which you give by loading a report and which you can withdraw at any time by deleting the data from the app.

5. Where the data lives, and how it is protected

In the app's private folder, on this iPhone. The files are protected by iOS encryption with the complete class: when the phone is locked they cannot be read even by connecting it to a computer.

You can add the Face ID or passcode lock: it is a PRO feature, and it protects against the most likely case, which is not a stolen phone but someone picking it up to look at a photo.

6. No connection

Synesix makes no network requests of any kind: no server, no account, no usage analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no third-party SDK.

Report text is recognised on the phone by Apple's Vision framework. Analyte explanations come from a catalogue inside the app. No language model generates text while you read, and no part of what you load is sent anywhere.

7. The widget

The widget shows how many values are out of range in the latest report and the collection date: two numbers and a date, and no value and no analyte name. It reads them from a small area shared between the app and the widget, on your device.

The reason for this choice is that the widget is visible on the lock screen, where anyone picking up the phone can see it.

8. Notifications

The reminder to repeat a test is scheduled on the device and only activates if you ask for it. The notification text contains no value from the report: it is read on the lock screen.

9. Export and report

You can export your data as JSON or CSV and generate a PDF report to take to the doctor. Export is free and stays free, including for users who never buy PRO.

Export only starts when you ask for it, and the file goes where you decide: share sheet, files, print. From that moment the file is outside the app and depends on where you put it.

10. Deleting data

You can delete a single report from the history, or all data from settings. Deletion is immediate and final: we keep no copies anywhere, so we cannot undo it.

11. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data. Since the data is only on your device and we receive none of it, you exercise these rights directly in the app: you see the data, correct it, export it and delete it.

If you believe the processing does not comply with the law you can contact the supervisory authority of your country.

12. Minors

Synesix is not intended for children under 14 and does not knowingly collect data from minors. If a parent or guardian believes a minor has used the app, they can delete the data directly from the device.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Substantial changes are flagged inside the app on the next launch.
